Color Palette Overview
Blue Family (Primary Brand)

Blue 900 - Deepest blue for high emphasis
Blue 700 - Dark blue for primary actions
Blue 600 - Standard blue for interactive elements
Blue 500 - Light blue for hover/active states
Blue 300 - Subtle blue for backgrounds/dividers

Sand Family (Warm Neutrals)

Sand 700 - Dark warm tone for text/emphasis
Sand 500 - Medium warm tone for secondary text
Sand 300 - Light warm tone for borders/dividers
Sand 200 - Very light for card backgrounds
Sand 100 - Lightest for page backgrounds

Orange (Accent)

Orange 200 - Warm accent for highlights/notifications

Neutral Family (Cool Grays)

Neutral 900 - Primary text color (nearly black)
Neutral 700 - Secondary text color
Neutral 400 - Tertiary text/disabled states
